Remote Support Workflow
  1. Send a short problem summary and your preferred availability window.
  2. Confirm the best remote support method and prepare access details.
  3. Run diagnostics (AnyDesk, ipconfig, ping) and identify the root cause.
  4. Apply the fix, verify the result, and walk you through the changes.
  5. Document the outcome and share follow-up recommendations.
