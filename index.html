Remote Support Workflow
  1. Email a short problem summary and availability window.
  2. Confirm the preferred remote method and prepare access details.
  3. Perform diagnostics (AnyDesk, ipconfig, ping) and identify root cause.
  4. Apply fix, verify results, and walk you through any changes.
  5. Document the outcome and provide follow-up recommendations.
